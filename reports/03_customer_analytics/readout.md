# SQL customer analytics

541,909 original rows → 524,878 positive-sale lines. Complete months end before 2011-12-01: 19,141 orders and 4,297 identified customers.

Gross sales include anonymous purchases; cohorts and RFM require known IDs. Returns are excluded, so sales are not net revenue or profit. Future cohort cells remain missing; observed zero-repeat cells are zero. Earliest invoice timestamp handles real minute-varying line times. RFM uses illustrative fixed thresholds. Customer-level RFM exports remain local. Associations do not establish causality.
